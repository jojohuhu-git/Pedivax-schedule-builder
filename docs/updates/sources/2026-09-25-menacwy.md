## The AAP/CDC disagreement on MenQuadfi's minimum age — AAP governs

CDC 2025 (quoted above): 2 years. AAP, via Washington State DOH's bulletin
(fetched live 2026-09-22 by the MeningoVax session, reused here):

> Updated MenACWY recommendations to align with the American Academy of
> Pediatrics. The minimum age for the first MenQuadfi dose is now 6 weeks,
> instead of 2 years for children who meet the recommendation.

Per CLAUDE.md's authority rule ("never adopt a CDC revision recommending
fewer doses or narrower eligibility than AAP"), AAP's 6-week floor governs
— MeningoVax already shipped this; this app matches it (docs/decisions.md,
"Two places AAP and CDC 2025 actually disagree", item 2). MenQuadfi's own
FDA insert (below) independently confirms 6 weeks, so this isn't even a
case of following AAP over the product's actual licence — CDC's 2-year
figure is the outlier here, not MenQuadfi's insert.

## Menveo package insert (DailyMed, label revision 3/2025)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=2dc730d5-55fd-4e98-8c8a-daa7d8f872b0&type=display

> MENVEO is a vaccine indicated for active immunization ... in individuals
> 2 months through 55 years of age.

> Adolescents and Adults Aged 11 through 55 Years: A single dose.

> Adolescents and Adults Aged 15 through 55 Years: A single booster dose
> of MENVEO ... may be administered to individuals who are at continued
> risk for meningococcal disease if at least 4 years have elapsed since a
> prior dose.

**Framing note, not a gap:** the insert's own booster language is written
around "continued risk," reflecting how the product's trials were framed,
not ACIP's later policy decision to recommend a routine 16-year booster
for every adolescent regardless of risk. ACIP/CDC's routine schedule
governs the *plan* (booster at 16 for everyone) — the insert's age window
(15-55) comfortably covers it, so there's no narrowing conflict, just an
insert written for a narrower original indication than current routine
policy uses it for.

## MenQuadfi package insert (DailyMed, label revision 4/2026)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=ab1c3318-578e-4d0c-a21e-fc6df07e9fb7&type=display

> MenQuadfi is approved for use in individuals 6 weeks of age and older.

> Individuals 2 years of age and older: A single dose.

> A single dose of MenQuadfi may be administered to individuals 13 years
> of age and older who are at continued risk for meningococcal disease if
> at least 3 years have elapsed since a prior dose.

Same framing note as Menveo — booster language is risk-based in the
insert, but the insert's own age floor (6 weeks) is what matters for
`minAgeDays`, and it independently confirms AAP's 6-week figure rather
than CDC's 2-year one. This session did not locate an explicit upper age
ceiling for MenQuadfi in the quoted sections (Menveo's insert states "55
years" directly; MenQuadfi's did not turn up in what was fetched) — recorded
as `maxAgeDays: null` rather than guessing a number to match Menveo's.

## Conclusion

MenACWY modeled as a 2-dose series (11-12 years, 16 years), no
`variants[]` — both products cover both doses identically for the routine
schedule this app plans. MenQuadfi's `minAgeDays` uses AAP's 6-week figure
(matching MeningoVax and its own insert), not CDC 2025's 2-year figure.
