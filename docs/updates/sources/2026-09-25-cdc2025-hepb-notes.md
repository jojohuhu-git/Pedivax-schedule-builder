## Quoted content (Hepatitis B, routine vaccination, mother HBsAg-negative)

> 3-dose series at ages 0, 1–2, and 6–18 months

> Birth weight ≥2,000 grams: 1 dose within 24 hours of birth if medically stable

> Birth weight <2,000 grams: 1 dose at chronological age 1 month or hospital discharge

> use monovalent HepB vaccine for doses administered before age 6 weeks

> Final (3rd or 4th) dose: age 6–18 months (minimum age 24 weeks)

> Administration of 4 doses is permitted when a combination vaccine containing HepB
> is used after the birth dose
