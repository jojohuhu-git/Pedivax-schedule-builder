# Snapshot: MenB — CDC schedule notes, and the October 2024 ACIP dosing change

Fetched live 2026-09-25 via the `verify-clinical-source` skill.

## CDC Child Immunization Schedule Notes, 2025 — MenB
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-notes.html

> Adolescents not at increased risk age 16–23 years (preferred age 16–18
> years) based on shared clinical decision-making.

> Bexsero or Trumenba (use same brand for all doses): 2-dose series at least
> 6 months apart

> if dose 2 is administered earlier than 6 months, administer dose 3 at
> least 4 months after dose 2

## Context: this is a pre-mid-2025 ACIP change, so it stands

A search turned up "New Dosing Interval and Schedule for the Bexsero MenB-4C
Vaccine: Updated Recommendations of the Advisory Committee on Immunization
Practices — United States, October 2024" (PMC11637420) — ACIP changed the
2-dose interval from 0-and-≥1-month to 0-and-6-months in **October 2024**.
Checked against docs/decisions.md's authority pin: the pin excludes federal
changes starting **mid-2025**; October 2024 predates that, so this change is
adopted, not recorded-and-ignored. It is already reflected in the CDC 2025
notes page quoted above (last reviewed July 2, 2025) — no separate action
needed, but worth writing down why this particular change is in rather than
one of the mid-2025-or-later ones this app deliberately excludes.

## Same-brand requirement — not dose-count tradeoff

Unlike Hib/Rotavirus, Bexsero and Trumenba both give a 2-dose series when
spaced correctly — there is no "shorter vs longer" choice here, only a
same-brand requirement ("use same brand for all doses"). Modeled as two
same-length variants in series.js rather than a fallback/shorter-vs-longer
pair.

## Conclusion

Both inserts agree exactly with CDC/ACIP (10-25 years licensed; 0-and-6-month
2-dose schedule for the healthy-adolescent population this app plans for) —
no insert-narrower conflict for either product.
