# Snapshot: HepB package inserts (Engerix-B, Recombivax HB)

Both fetched live 2026-09-25 via the `verify-clinical-source` skill (WebFetch;
direct FDA PDF fetch returned unreadable binary content in this sandbox, so the
DailyMed HTML mirror of each label was used instead).

## Engerix-B

**URL:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=4d930f24-4ddb-488d-9e79-3f495972733b&type=display
**Label revision shown:** 3/2010 — unusually old; **flag to re-check** against a
newer DailyMed setid before this app ships, the same way Prevnar 13's date is
flagged as unverified in docs/decisions.md.

> first dose: at elected date; second dose: 1 month later; third dose: 6 months
> after first dose

> Primary immunization for infants ... children (birth through 10 years) ...
> consists of a series of 3 doses (0.5 mL each) given on a 0-, 1-, and 6-month
> schedule.

## Recombivax HB

**URL:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=f1ad4bca-839d-41cd-a132-a6984780912e&type=display
**Label revision shown:** 6/2021

> A series of 3 doses (0.5 mL each) given on a 0-, 1-, and 6-month schedule.

> Hepatitis B vaccination should be delayed until 1 month of age or hospital
> discharge in infants weighing <2000 g if the mother is documented to be
> HBsAg negative.

## Conclusion

Both inserts allow from birth, agreeing with CDC 2025 / AAP 2026 — no
insert-narrower-than-organization conflict for either product.
