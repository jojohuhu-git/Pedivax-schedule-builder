## CDC Child Immunization Schedule Notes, 2025 — Hib
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-notes.html

> ActHIB, Hiberix, Pentacel, or Vaxelis: 4-dose series (3-dose primary series
> at age 2, 4, and 6 months, followed by a booster dose at age 12–15 months)

> Vaxelis is not recommended for use as a booster dose.

> PedvaxHIB: 3-dose series (2-dose primary series at age 2 and 4 months,
> followed by a booster dose at age 12–15 months)

> American Indian and Alaska Native infants: Vaxelis and PedvaxHIB preferred
> over other Hib vaccines for the primary series. (Recorded for completeness;
> out of scope for v1 — healthy children, no risk-group branching.)

> Minimum age for dose 1: 6 weeks

> Dose 3 to dose 4 (booster): 8 weeks (as final dose). This dose only
> necessary for children age 12 through 59 months who received 3 doses
> before the 1st birthday.

## PedvaxHIB package insert (DailyMed, label revision April 2023)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9dff46c6-4b15-4d10-aca6-d5ef3735a530

> Infants 2 to 14 months of age should receive a 0.5 mL dose of vaccine
> ideally beginning at 2 months of age followed by a 0.5 mL dose 2 months later

> In infants completing the primary two-dose regimen before 12 months of age,
> a booster dose (0.5 mL) should be administered at 12 to 15 months of age,
> but not earlier than 2 months after the second dose.

> Liquid PedvaxHIB IS NOT RECOMMENDED FOR USE IN INFANTS YOUNGER THAN 6 WEEKS OF AGE

## Insert-vs-organization gap found here

**ActHIB and Hiberix's own inserts put the booster at 15-18 months; CDC/ACIP's
routine recommendation allows it as early as 12 months.** PedvaxHIB's insert
already says 12-15 months and agrees with CDC exactly. Per the authority
rule, CDC/ACIP governs — a 12-month booster with ActHIB or Hiberix is valid
even though it is earlier than what those two inserts alone would suggest.
Recorded as a `facts[]` entry on each product, not enforced as a narrower gate.
