## CDC Child Immunization Schedule Notes, 2025 — HPV
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-notes.html
Page last reviewed: July 2, 2025

> HPV vaccination routinely recommended at age 11–12 years (can start at
> age 9 years)

> Age 9–14 years at initial vaccination: 2-dose series at 0, 6–12 months
> (minimum interval: 5 months; repeat dose if administered too soon)

> Age 15 years or older at initial vaccination: 3-dose series at 0, 1–2
> months, 6 months

This app's plan always starts a healthy child's HPV series in the 9-14
band (11- and 12-year visits, per decisions.md), so only the 2-dose branch
above ever applies — the 15+/3-dose branch is CDC's rule for someone whose
*first* dose comes after turning 15, which can't happen in an on-time
birth-to-18 plan. Not modeled.

**No AAP/CDC disagreement here, unlike decisions.md's framing implied** —
decisions.md attributed the 2-dose-at-0/6-12-months rule to AAP
specifically ("AAP gives 2 doses at 0 and 6–12 months"), but the CDC 2025
notes page quoted above states the identical rule for the same age band.
Both organizations agree; citing CDC here per CLAUDE.md's "cite either, no
decision needed" rule (this session read the CDC page directly; the AAP
PDF returned unparseable binary again, same known friction as prior
sessions' HepB/other AAP-PDF attempts).

## Gardasil 9 package insert (DailyMed, label revision 3/2025)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=a21f4f4b-b891-4f25-b747-cb9ec7d865d6&type=display
Licensed age range: 9 through 45 years (both sexes).

> 0, 6 to 12 months

> If the second dose is administered earlier than 5 months after the
> first dose, administer a third dose at least 4 months after the second
> dose.

Matches CDC/ACIP exactly — no insert-narrower conflict. Gardasil 9 is the
only currently-marketed HPV product (Gardasil-quadrivalent and Cervarix
are both discontinued in the US; not added since they're not on the
mockup's formulary list and this app models what a clinic can actually
stock today).

## Conclusion

Modeled as a plain 2-dose series at 0 and 6-12 months, planned at the
11-year and 12-year visits per decisions.md. The 5-month-minimum/
repeat-if-too-soon rule is recorded as a fact but doesn't bind an on-time
plan (11 and 12 years are exactly 12 months apart, well over the 5-month
floor).
