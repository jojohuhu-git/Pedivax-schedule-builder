# Snapshot: DTaP — CDC schedule notes, catch-up intervals, and both
# single-antigen products' inserts

Fetched live 2026-09-25 via the `verify-clinical-source` skill.

## CDC Child Immunization Schedule Notes, 2025 — DTaP
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-notes.html
Page last reviewed: July 2, 2025

> 5-dose series (3-dose primary series at age 2, 4, and 6 months, followed
> by booster doses at ages 15–18 months and 4–6 years)

> Dose 4 may be administered as early as age 12 months if at least 6 months
> have elapsed since dose 3.

The page does not give brand-lineage guidance for the two 4-6-year booster
products (Kinrix, Quadracel) — that's confirmed separately below, reusing
docs/decisions.md's existing verification.

## CDC child/adolescent catch-up table — DTaP minimum intervals
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/schedules/wcms-inc/child-schedule-table-catchup_TP4.html

> Minimum age for dose 1: 6 weeks

> Dose 1 to dose 2: 4 weeks

> Dose 2 to dose 3: 4 weeks

> Dose 3 to dose 4: 6 months

> Dose 4 to dose 5: 6 months

> A fifth dose is not necessary if the fourth dose was administered at age 4
> years or older and at least 6 months after dose 3

That exception can't occur in this app's on-time plan (dose 4 always lands
at 15-18 months, well before age 4), so it's not modeled as a schedule
branch — same treatment as PCV's equivalent dose-4 exception.

## Daptacel package insert (DailyMed, label revision 8/2026)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=06f34d0f-4e72-41d3-967f-8abf3f2005c1
Licensed age range: infants and children 6 weeks through 6 years of age
(prior to 7th birthday).

> The five dose immunization series consists of a 0.5 mL dose administered
> intramuscularly at 2, 4, 6 and 15-20 months of age, and at 4-6 years of
> age.

> The first dose may be given as early as 6 weeks of age.

## Infanrix package insert (DailyMed, label revision 5/2026)
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=de16dd6a-859b-4180-c6af-f930be14f26a&type=display
Licensed age range: infants and children aged 6 weeks through 6 years
(prior to the seventh birthday).

> One dose each at 2, 4, and 6 months of age. One booster dose at 15 to 20
> months of age and another booster dose at 4 to 6 years of age.

Both inserts say dose 4 at **15-20** months, one month wider than CDC/ACIP's
15-18-month routine window. This is the insert being *wider*, not narrower
— no conflict under the "insert may fill a gap, never narrow a rule" rule
(CLAUDE.md), so nothing needs recording as a gap the way Rotavirus/Hib were.
CDC/ACIP's 15-18 months governs the on-time schedule regardless.

## Kinrix / Quadracel — deliberately NOT added this commit

Both are DTaP+IPV combination products. Per the B2 queue
(docs/archive/handoff-2026-09-25-b1-b2-partial.md), a combo product is only
added after every antigen it covers is verified — IPV is not done yet. The
lineage rule for these two (which earlier brands each is licensed to
follow, and that lineage is a preference, not a bar) is already live-
verified and quoted in docs/decisions.md, "Found while mocking up" section,
2026-09-24 — reuse those quotes when IPV is done, don't re-fetch.

## Conclusion

DTaP modeled as a plain 5-dose series (like HepB/PCV), no `variants[]` —
Daptacel and Infanrix both cover doses 1-5 identically, no brand choice that
changes series length. Both inserts agree with CDC/ACIP (insert's dose-4
window is wider, not narrower — no gap to record).
