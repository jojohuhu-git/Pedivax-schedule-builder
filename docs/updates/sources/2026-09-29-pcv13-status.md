# PCV13 (Prevnar 13) — current status and retirement date

Fetched live 2026-09-29 while settling the owner's decision that "a year is sufficient"
for Prevnar 13's retirement date. Three sources were tried; what each actually says is
below, verbatim.

## immunize.org — Ask the Experts: Pneumococcal (organization tier)

URL: http://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/pneumococcal/
Fetched live 2026-09-29.

> PCV13 (Prevnar 13, Pfizer) is FDA-licensed and may still be available in some clinics.
> It is no longer routinely recommended.

Also on the same page:

> ACIP no longer recommends PCV13 for children or adults; however, PCV13 may be given as
> previously recommended if it is the only PCV available and the recipient would
> otherwise go without vaccination.

**This is the load-bearing sentence.** It is why `cover.js` refuses to plan with PCV13,
and it is an organization-tier source. Note what it does *not* say: it gives no
retirement date, and it does not say the product was withdrawn — only that it is no
longer routinely recommended.

## CDC — Pneumococcal surveillance (organization tier)

URL: https://www.cdc.gov/pneumococcal/php/surveillance/index.html
Fetched live 2026-09-29.

States only when PCV13 was introduced:

> PCV13: 2010 in children; 2012 in adults with risk conditions; 2014-2019 in adults 65
> years or older.

No discontinuation date. Checked explicitly.

## Pfizer — Prevnar 20 adult site (manufacturer marketing, no tier in this app)

URL: https://adult.prevnar20.com/whyprevnar20
Fetched live 2026-09-29 (also fetched 2026-09-26, same sentence).

> In the US, Prevnar 13 was available for adults from 2012 to 2024.

This is the **only** source found in five sessions that names a year. Two caveats kept
with it: it is a manufacturer marketing page, not an organization source or an insert
(which is why it is named in prose rather than added to `sources.js`, whose tiers are
`organization` and `insert` only); and the sentence is about **adults**, while this app
plans children.

## The day-level date is abandoned, by owner decision

"30 April 2024" was carried in the data with a caveat across four sessions. It rests on a
Medline PDF that returns HTTP 403 to every tool tried (WebFetch, curl, and a real browser
session) — an active bot-verification wall, not a dead link. Owner decided 2026-09-29:
**ignore the exact date, a year is sufficient.** Do not re-add a day without a sentence
read live from an organization source.
