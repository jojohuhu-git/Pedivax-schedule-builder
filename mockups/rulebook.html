Which authority this app follows, and why the edition matters. Federal vaccine policy changed repeatedly from mid-2025 onwards. This app is pinned to guidance as it stood before those changes: the CDC 2025 schedule, alongside AAP, which has gone on publishing its own schedule and keeps every routine childhood vaccine routine. Where AAP and CDC disagree, AAP governs — two such disagreements are marked below. Every quote names its edition and its date, so you can always see which vintage a rule is.

What this rulebook does not cover yet

  • Healthy children only. No asplenia, HIV, complement deficiency, transplant or other risk conditions.
  • No catch-up rules. Every rule here assumes the child starts from nothing and stays on time.
  • Influenza, COVID-19 and RSV are annual or season-dependent and sit outside the birth-to-18 plan.
  • Minimum intervals between doses are only listed where the source states one. Full interval tables come with the real data file.
